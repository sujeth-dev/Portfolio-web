01 \u2014 Adaptive Learning System
SynthesisLive / Solo
Most platforms move every student through the same fixed curriculum.
Synthesis adapts learning paths based on what a learner actually knows. As learners answer questions, mastery estimates continuously update and future concepts unlock dynamically. It combines:
  • Knowledge graphs to model prerequisite relationships
  • Bayesian Knowledge Tracing to estimate mastery
  • SM-2 spaced repetition for long-term retention
Why it matters
Students spend less time repeating concepts they've already mastered, and more time where learning is actually needed.

Live demoCode
The idea
Treat a curriculum like a dependency graph, not a playlist. You can't learn X before its prerequisites \u2014 so model that explicitly and route each learner through what they actually need.
How it works
  • Prerequisite DAG \u2014 concepts as nodes, dependencies as edges, laid out with dagre + React Flow.
  • BKT \u2014 probabilistic per-concept mastery that updates with every answer.
  • SM-2 \u2014 schedules reviews so retention compounds instead of cramming.
Why it's hard
The three have to agree: mastery feeds what unlocks next, which feeds what gets scheduled. Making the loop coherent \u2014 not three features bolted together \u2014 was the real work.
