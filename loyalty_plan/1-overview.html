How it works

Scan. Earn. Redeem.
Repeat — by design.

Step 01

Customer scans the QR.

A branded mobile page opens — no app, no download. They enter their phone number. WhatsApp OTP. Two taps in, they see their progress already at 1 of 5. Their first visit just counted.

Step 02

Habit compounds, silently.

Every scan moves them along the loop. WhatsApp nudges fire at 60% and 80%. Off-peak visits double the progress. Birthday triggers fire on the day. Friends they refer earn for both sides automatically.

Step 03

Reward unlocks. Loop restarts.

A 6-digit code. Customer shows it. Staff types it in counter mode — green tick in five seconds. A new loop unlocks immediately. There is never an empty state. The journey is always alive.
