All Engines Architecture Integrations Decisions Roadmap
09 · Phased roadmap

What ships when.
And what unlocks what.

Three phases. Each adds capability without rebuilding what came before. Every engine evolves on its own track — and integration is the thread that unlocks everything else.

Phase 1 · MVP1
3 months

Lean MVP. QR + WhatsApp + counter mode. 5 design-partner restaurants in Bangalore.

Phase 2 · Scale
Month 4–8

POS integration, segmentation, premium tier. 20–30 paying restaurants.

Phase 3 · Network
Month 9–18

Auto-verification, multi-city, cross-restaurant discovery. 100+ restaurants.

Target by month 18
₹15L MRR

100 restaurants on Pro tier average, modest premium expansion.

Phase 1 · ships now Phase 2 · next 4–8 mo Phase 3 · 9+ mo / deferred
