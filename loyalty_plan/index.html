How to use this

Each file is self-contained and deployable on its own. Send #03 to a restaurant. Open #02 in a working session. Hand #06 to an engineer. They cross-link but never depend.

What's the spine

Eight engines: Identity, Customisation, Reward, Flow, Verification, Communication, Integration, Analytics. Present minimally everywhere — central in #02 and #06.

What's the wedge

Restaurants own their customer data. Loop runs on WhatsApp, not SMS. Per-restaurant theming, not platform-branded. Anti-aggregator stance.
