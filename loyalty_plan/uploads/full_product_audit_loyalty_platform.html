What your customers actually feel

Critical gap
The "why bother" moment at first scan
When a customer scans a QR cold, they see a sign-up form with zero context. No reason to care. India's Gen Z and millennial diners make a split-second judgment: "is this worth my number?" Your current concept has no described instant hook. Research shows the reward must be obvious within seconds of first interaction — not after completing a journey.
Fix
Show a specific, tangible reward on the very first screen before sign-up. "Scan to unlock a free cold brew on your 2nd visit" beats "Join our loyalty program" every time. Make the value visible before they give anything.
Critical gap
Habit loops need a trigger, not just a reward
Your milestone system (5 coffees → free coffee) is the right structure, but habits are formed by: Trigger → Action → Variable reward. The trigger is missing. Without push/WhatsApp reminders that say "You're 1 coffee away from your free one", customers forget the loop exists between visits. Duolingo's streak mechanic works because it reminds you daily — your program needs the same.
Fix
Build milestone proximity triggers: WhatsApp message fires at 60%, 80%, 100% of loop completion. "You've had 4 coffees — one more and your free one unlocks." This one mechanic can dramatically improve loop completion rates.
Needs attention
Social proof is missing from the customer journey
Indian diners are heavily influenced by what others are doing. Zomato leverages this brilliantly with ratings and "X people ordered this today." Your platform's customer-facing pages currently have no social signal — no "32 people unlocked this reward this week", no visible community. This is a missed emotional trigger.
Fix
Add lightweight social proof to reward pages: "18 members redeemed this week." It creates FOMO and validates the program's value without requiring a community feature to be built.
Needs attention
Screenshot verification kills momentum
Your plan includes activities requiring screenshot/proof verification. Research on loyalty UX is very clear: every extra step after completing an action reduces completion by 30–50%. If a customer posts a story, earns the right to a reward, and then has to screenshot + upload + wait for manual approval — most will abandon. The dopamine hit must be immediate.
Fix
Design for "instant gratification, async verification." Give an immediate provisional reward confirmation ("Your reward is being processed — you'll get it in 2 hours") rather than blocking the experience. Or auto-verify via Instagram URL sharing instead of screenshot.
Strength
Gamification structure is psychologically sound
The evolving journey concept — where new loops unlock after completion — taps directly into "endowed progress" and "status protection" psychology. Once a customer completes a loop and unlocks a new journey, they have something to lose. This is far more powerful than static punch cards. Starbucks built an empire on exactly this mechanic.

Retention science applied to your product

Critical
Month 1 drop-off — your biggest enemy
Research shows the steepest loyalty program churn happens in the first 30 days. Customers sign up, get distracted, and forget the program exists. Your current concept has no defined "re-engagement" mechanic for lapsed early users. The WhatsApp campaign system is listed as a future feature — but it's actually the most critical retention tool you have for Month 1.
Fix — Day 1 / Day 7 / Day 14 playbook
Day 1: WhatsApp welcome with their first milestone progress shown visually.
Day 7: "You're halfway to your first reward" nudge if no activity.
Day 14: "Your reward is about to expire" urgency message.
This automated sequence should be in your MVP, not a later phase.
Critical
No win-back mechanism for lapsed customers
Indian restaurants using WhatsApp marketing report 40% higher repeat visit rates from re-engagement campaigns. Customers who haven't visited in 30+ days are a recoverable segment — but only if you identify them and act. Your current concept has no defined lapsed-user flow. Reelo does this, and it's one of their most used features by restaurants.
Fix
Build a "30/60/90 day lapsed" trigger in the admin. Restaurants can send a single personalised comeback offer: "We miss you! Here's a ₹100 off your next visit." Auto-identifies the segment, restaurant approves and sends. Simple. High-ROI.
Needs attention
Rewards that feel too far away cause abandonment
If a customer needs 5 visits to get a free coffee but visits once a month, that's a 5-month wait. Research is clear: if rewards feel unreachable, customers disengage after 2–3 attempts. Your milestone system needs to account for visit frequency — a casual diner needs a shorter first loop than a regular.
Fix
Let restaurants set a "quick win" — a smaller reward on the 2nd visit to keep early momentum, then the bigger milestone loop kicks in. First reward in 2 visits max. This is the single biggest driver of early retention in loyalty programs.
Needs attention
Activity fatigue — too many actions dilutes all of them
Your activity list is long: review, story, reel, referral, birthday, check-in, event. If a customer sees 8 activities at once, they do nothing. Paradox of choice is real. The best gamified programs show 2–3 active missions at a time — complete them, new ones unlock.
Fix
Show maximum 3 active activities at once per customer. Rotate them based on what they haven't done yet. This keeps the dashboard fresh and actions achievable.
Strength
Evolving journey loops are your biggest retention weapon
The concept of journeys that evolve after completion is your strongest retention mechanic. Once a customer has completed 2 loops and unlocked a third, they have invested identity and progress in your program — research calls this "sunk cost loyalty." They won't abandon it. Duolingo calls this the "streak effect." Protect this mechanic — it's your moat.

Business model analysis

Risk
One-time setup + 6-month contract creates a cash problem
You collect setup fee upfront then a flat retainer. After 6 months, renewal is uncertain. If 30% of restaurants don't renew, your revenue is unpredictable and you can't plan headcount or product development. This is the most common startup cash flow mistake in B2B SaaS.
Recommended model
Setup fee (₹10k–20k) = one-time, covers onboarding + branding config.
Monthly retainer (₹4k–8k/month) = rolling after 6-month lock.
Add-ons = extra outlets (₹2k each), WhatsApp credits, campaign boosts.
This gives you predictable MRR and upsell opportunities.
Consider
Direct sales doesn't scale past 30 restaurants alone
Direct B2B sales works for your first 10–15 restaurants — you need those to learn anyway. But each restaurant sale takes 2–4 weeks of demos, follow-ups, and onboarding. At 1 salesperson you're capped at 5–7 new restaurants per month. You need channel multipliers early.
GTM additions to plan
1. F&B consultants and restaurant marketing agencies as partners (10–15% referral fee).
2. Integrate with popular Bangalore POS (Petpooja, UrbanPiper) — restaurants see you as a native add-on.
3. Instagram/LinkedIn content showing real restaurant results — inbound from restaurant owners who see ROI data.
Opportunity
The Zomato/Swiggy pain is your sales hook
Reelo's CEO said it best: "Rising third-party commissions, inconsistent footfalls, and shrinking margins." CCI found both Zomato and Swiggy guilty of antitrust violations in Jan 2025. Bangalore restaurant owners are actively looking for ways to reduce aggregator dependency. Your platform directly addresses this — own the customer relationship. This is your sales narrative, not just a feature list.
Pitch reframe
"Stop renting your customers from Zomato. Build your own loyal base that comes back to you directly." Lead with this in every sales conversation — it speaks to a real, active pain point in every Bangalore restaurant owner's life right now.
Opportunity
WhatsApp API is a revenue line, not just a feature
WhatsApp Business API costs money per message (Meta charges per conversation). Indian restaurants using WhatsApp marketing report 3–5x ROI. You can bundle a WhatsApp message credit system into your pricing — restaurants buy credits, you handle the API, you take a margin. This turns a cost center into a recurring revenue stream.
Model
Include 500 WhatsApp messages/month in the base plan. Additional messages sold as credits at ₹0.50–₹1 per message. For a restaurant with 1,000 customers sending 2 campaigns/month, that's 2,000 messages × ₹0.80 = ₹1,600/month in add-on revenue per restaurant.
Watch out
Restaurants will ask "what's my ROI?" on renewal day
At month 5, before renewal, a restaurant owner will look at what they spent (₹40–60k over 6 months) and ask: "Did this work?" If you can't show them specific numbers — how many customers returned, how many rewards redeemed, revenue attributed — they won't renew. This is where most SMB SaaS companies lose accounts.
Fix — build the ROI report early
A simple monthly "Your loyalty summary" sent to admins via WhatsApp or email: X new members, Y return visits, Z rewards redeemed, estimated revenue from loyalty customers. Makes renewal a no-brainer conversation.

Missing features — prioritized by impact

Build in MVP
Automated WhatsApp trigger system
Not campaigns (which are manual one-time sends) — automated triggers based on customer behavior. This is the engine of your retention system. Without it, the platform is passive. India's WhatsApp open rates are 85–95% vs 20% email — this is your highest-leverage channel.
Triggers to build first
• Welcome message on sign-up (immediate)
• Milestone proximity: 1 step away from reward
• Reward unlocked notification
• 7-day inactivity nudge
• Birthday message with offer (7 days before)
Build in MVP
Staff-side redemption app / screen
This is consistently the most overlooked part of loyalty platform design — and the one that kills programs at the restaurant level. If staff can't verify a coupon in under 5 seconds on a busy Friday night, they skip it, customers feel embarrassed, and the program dies from the inside. Your admin panel exists, but does it have a simplified staff view?
Fix
Build a dedicated "counter mode" — a single-screen view optimized for staff, showing only: scan/enter code → confirm → done. Nothing else. Works on any phone. No login complexity. Separate from the full admin panel.
Build in MVP
Onboarding wizard for restaurants
You plan to onboard restaurants via direct sales. But once sold, someone has to set up the restaurant's branding, rewards, milestones. If this requires you to do it manually every time, you can't scale past 20 restaurants. A self-serve setup wizard (even basic) makes onboarding a product, not a service.
Minimum viable wizard
Step 1: Upload logo + pick brand colors.
Step 2: Choose milestone template (coffee shop / casual dining / bar).
Step 3: Set first reward.
Step 4: QR code generated and ready.
Live in under 30 minutes without your team touching it.
Phase 2
Activity auto-verification via Instagram
Your manual screenshot verification will break at scale. Instagram's API allows story/post URL sharing — customers can share a direct link instead of a screenshot, which your system can log (even if not auto-verify). This reduces the verification burden without requiring full automation immediately.
Phase 2
Customer segmentation in admin
Right now campaigns seem to go to all customers. The highest-ROI campaigns are segmented: lapsed 30 days, high-value regulars, birthday this week, completed a journey. Even 3–4 basic segments would make the campaign feature dramatically more useful — and this is something Reelo does well.
Differentiator — protect this
Per-restaurant brand theming
This is your clearest moat vs Reelo and other Indian competitors. A Koramangala brewpub's loyalty page should feel nothing like a Indiranagar cafe's. Full color, typography, imagery, and tone customization. No other Indian competitor does this well. Don't compromise it to ship faster — it's the reason premium restaurants will choose you over Reelo.
Future — high value
Cross-restaurant discovery layer
As you onboard more Bangalore restaurants, you'll have a unique asset: a customer who's loyal to Restaurant A on your platform might discover Restaurant B also on your platform nearby. A "restaurants near you on [platform name]" feature turns your network into a discovery engine — and gives you a powerful upsell story to restaurants: "Join and get exposure to our member base."
