# Placement Readiness: 13-Week Execution Plan

> 1 hr/day DSA, every day. Pattern-by-pattern. Skip what you know. 55 done → 200+ by end of summer.

---

## Weekly Structure

Each week:
- **Mon–Sat:** 3 problems/day (1 easy + 2 mediums) = 18/week
- **Sunday:** Review week. Redo 1–2 problems you got wrong. 30 min behavioral prep.

One pattern per week or two weeks depending on depth. Move on when you can solve mediums without looking at solutions.

## Week 11 — Aug 12–18: DP Part 1 (1D)

## Week 13 — Aug 26–31: Review + Mock + Behavioral Final

**Problems target:** 12 (mixed review — pick your weakest patterns)
**Focus:** Drill any pattern still shaky. No new patterns.

**Mock interviews #3–5:** Get 3 done this week if possible. Include behavioral round (ask someone to give you a 30-min behavioral mock — just the stories).

**Behavioral:** Run through all 6 stories verbally. Record yourself. Under 2 min each.

**System design:** Review your notes on all 5 topics. Can you draw each one on paper in 5 min?
