# Freelance: 13-Week Execution Plan

> Retain current clients. Experiment with 2 industries in Weeks 1–4. Double down on what works. One Tier 2 pursuit running in parallel from Week 3.

---

## Week 1 — Jun 4–8: Setup + Client Confirmation

- [ ] Fill in client table in blueprint (name, service, income, check-in day)
- [ ] Confirm all current client work scope and upcoming deadlines
- [ ] Send Friday update to all retainer clients (use template from blueprint)
- [ ] Invoice any outstanding work
- [ ] Build Tier 1 target list: 10 schools/tutoring centres + 10 field service businesses near you (Google Maps research, 30 min)

**Milestone:** Current client situation fully documented. 20 Tier 1 targets identified.

---

## Week 2 — Jun 9–15: First Outreach Batch

**Current clients:** Normal delivery + Friday update.

**Tier 1 outreach (10 total this week):**
- 5 schools/tutoring centres (walk-in or cold call, Tue–Thu afternoon)
- 5 field service businesses (cold WhatsApp using template from blueprint)

**Log every contact:** business name, method, response, next action.

**Tier 2:** Research which manufacturing / franchise / jewellery businesses you have any personal access to. Ask in your network: "Do you know anyone who runs a manufacturing or franchise business?"

**Milestone:** 10 Tier 1 contacts made. 1 personal connection for Tier 2 identified (even a weak one).

---

## Week 3 — Jun 16–22: Follow-Up + Tier 2 Entry

**Tier 1:**
- Follow up on all Week 2 contacts (Day 7)
- 10 new contacts (same split: 5 schools, 5 field service)
- Any interested contact → schedule demo call or in-person visit

**Tier 2:**
- Book 1 discovery meeting (not a sales call — "I'm researching how [manufacturers/franchises] manage operations. Can I come see your workflow for 30 min?")

**Milestone:** 20 Tier 1 contacts total. 1 Tier 2 discovery meeting done.

---

## Week 4 — Jun 23–30: First Close Attempt + Industry Decision

**Tier 1:**
- For any warm leads (2+ conversations): offer to show a live demo or prototype
- 5 new contacts in the better-performing industry
- 5 contacts in a NEW industry (try cooperative societies or NGOs — LinkedIn outreach)

**Review after Week 4:**
- Which industry had the most responses? (Schools? Field service? Other?)
- Which outreach method worked? (Walk-in? WhatsApp? Email?)
- **Double down on the one industry + method that converted best. Drop the one that didn't.**

**Tier 2:** Second discovery meeting or follow-up from Week 3.

**Milestone:** At least 1 warm Tier 1 lead in active demo/close conversation. Industry decision made.

---

## Weeks 5–8 — Jul 1–28: Double Down + First Tier 1 Close

**Weekly Tier 1 routine:**
- 10 contacts/week in your chosen winning industry
- Follow up all 7-day-old entries
- Any demo scheduled → close it with the 3-month free offer

**Monthly invoice:** Send on Jul 1st for all retainer clients.

**Tier 2:** Proposal drafted based on discovery meeting findings.

**Milestone (Week 8):** 1 Tier 1 client signed (retainer or project). First additional income stream beyond current clients.

---

## Weeks 9–11 — Aug 1–18: Second Tier 1 Close + Tier 2 Proposal

**Tier 1:**
- Continue 10 contacts/week
- Close 2nd new client

**Tier 2:**
- Send proposal to Tier 2 prospect
- Follow up every 10 days
- These close slowly — be patient and persistent

**Milestone (Week 11):** 2 new Tier 1 clients. Tier 2 proposal in negotiation.

---

## Weeks 12–13 — Aug 19–31: Wrap, Document, Scale Plan

**Tier 1:**
- Clients onboarded and running smoothly
- Document what you built (for portfolio + freelance references)

**Tier 2:**
- If no close yet — maintain the relationship. These deals take 4–12 weeks from first meeting. Don't abandon.

**Freelance portfolio notes:**
- One paragraph per client engagement (for resume and portfolio)
- Ask happy clients: "Would you be willing to say a quick word about working with me? I'd use it on my portfolio with your permission."

**Milestone (Aug 31):**
- [ ] Current clients retained
- [ ] 1–2 new Tier 1 clients added
- [ ] Tier 2 in active conversation (even if not closed)
- [ ] Monthly freelance income stable or increased vs June
- [ ] 2–3 portfolio case study paragraphs written

---

## Industry Outreach Tracker Structure

Set up a simple log (Google Sheets, Notion, or plain MD):

| Date | Business | Industry | Method | Response | Status | Next action |
|------|---------|---------|--------|---------|--------|------------|
| | | | Walk-in/WhatsApp/Email | No response / Interested / Demo done / Signed | | |

Review every Saturday. Identify what's converting. Adjust approach weekly.
