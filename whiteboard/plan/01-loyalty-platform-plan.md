# Loop: 13-Week Execution Plan

> One thing at a time. Each week has a build focus, an outreach focus, and a binary milestone.

---

## Week 1 — Jun 4–8: Build Complete + Deploy Ready

**Build:**
- [ ] Complete all unchecked items in the MVP1 feature tracker (see blueprint)
- [ ] End-to-end flow tested locally: QR scan → OTP → welcome → visit → reward → counter redemption
- [ ] BullMQ workers deployed (Vercel Cron or Fly.io)
- [ ] All 7 WhatsApp templates submitted to Meta via BSP (Interakt/AiSensy)

**Outreach:** None yet. Focus 100% on shipping.

**Milestone:** End-to-end flow works without errors on local dev.

---

## Week 2 — Jun 9–15: Live Deployment

**Build:**
- [ ] Deploy to Vercel (production, not preview)
- [ ] Supabase production project provisioned, schema migrated
- [ ] Environment variables set in Vercel
- [ ] Custom domain connected
- [ ] WhatsApp templates approved by Meta
- [ ] Razorpay switched to production mode
- [ ] Sentry live, capturing test errors
- [ ] Full deployment checklist (from blueprint) — every item checked

**Outreach:** None. Fix any bugs that appear after deployment.

**Milestone:** Loop is live at your domain. A stranger can scan the test QR and complete the full flow.

---

## Week 3 — Jun 16–22: First Restaurant Contacts

**Build:**
- [ ] Fix any bugs from Week 2
- [ ] Polish onboarding wizard (first impression matters)
- [ ] Create one printed demo QR card to bring to walk-ins

**Outreach:**
- [ ] Build target list: 15 restaurants (Google Maps + Zomato research, see blueprint)
- [ ] 5 walk-in visits (Koramangala or Indiranagar, Tue–Thu 2–5pm)
- [ ] 5 cold WhatsApp messages (for those not visited in person)
- [ ] Log every contact: name, response, next action

**Milestone:** 10 restaurants contacted. At least 2 expressed interest ("tell me more" or "let's talk").

---

## Week 4 — Jun 23–30: First Design Partner Signed

**Build:**
- [ ] Address any onboarding issues surfaced from demo conversations
- [ ] Make sure counter mode works well on mobile (staff will use their phones)

**Outreach:**
- [ ] Follow up on all Week 3 contacts (Day 7 follow-up)
- [ ] 5 new walk-ins + 5 new WhatsApp contacts
- [ ] For interested restaurants: go back with a live demo (on your phone)
- [ ] Close at least 1 design partner (3 months free offer)

**Milestone (binary):** 1 design partner signed and onboarding session scheduled.

---

## Week 5 — Jul 1–7: First Restaurant Live

**Build:**
- [ ] Sit with first restaurant for onboarding session (30 min, co-sit)
- [ ] QR code printed and on their counter
- [ ] Staff trained on counter mode
- [ ] Owner knows how to access dashboard

**Outreach:**
- [ ] Continue: 5 new contacts this week
- [ ] Pipeline review: anyone from Weeks 3–4 still warm?

**Milestone:** First restaurant live. At least 3 real customer scans in Week 5.

---

## Week 6 — Jul 8–14: Second Design Partner + Fix Bugs

**Build:**
- [ ] Fix any bugs surfaced by first restaurant's live usage
- [ ] Implement any quick wins from their feedback (if valuable and small)
- [ ] Nothing that breaks the core loop — be conservative

**Outreach:**
- [ ] Close 2nd design partner
- [ ] Continue 5 new contacts/week
- [ ] Check in with first restaurant: how's it going? Any issues?

**Milestone:** 2 restaurants live. First restaurant has 10+ customer scans.

---

## Week 7 — Jul 15–21: Third Partner + First Data

**Build:**
- [ ] Check if verification queue SLAs (2h/6h) are being met
- [ ] Dashboard KPIs loading correctly for owners
- [ ] Win-back cron job running (verify with test customer at D30)

**Outreach:**
- [ ] Close 3rd design partner
- [ ] Begin probing first restaurant about paid conversion: "How's it been? What would you say to a friend who asked if it's worth it?"

**Milestone:** 3 restaurants live. First restaurant has used Loop for 30 days.

---

## Week 8 — Jul 22–28: First Paid Conversion Conversation

**Outreach:**
- [ ] Have an honest conversation with first design partner: "The free period ends in Month 3. I wanted to check in early — is this delivering value for you?"
- [ ] Show them their data: customers added, return visits, rewards redeemed
- [ ] If yes: introduce pricing (₹3,500/month Starter)
- [ ] Continue pipeline: 4th and 5th design partner targets

**Milestone:** First design partner says "yes, I'll pay" — even if the invoice isn't sent yet.

---

## Week 9 — Jul 29–Aug 4: 4th Partner + Case Study Draft

**Build:**
- [ ] Implement highest-impact feedback from first 3 restaurants
- [ ] Begin writing case study for Restaurant 1: before/after, data, quote

**Outreach:**
- [ ] Onboard 4th design partner
- [ ] Continue outreach to 5th

**Milestone:** Case study draft written. 4 restaurants live.

---

## Week 10 — Aug 5–11: 5th Partner + First Paid Invoice

**Outreach:**
- [ ] Onboard 5th design partner
- [ ] Send first paid invoice to Restaurant 1 (Starter: ₹3,500/month)
- [ ] Begin paid conversion conversations with Restaurant 2 and 3

**Milestone:** First ₹3,500 invoice sent and paid.

---

## Week 11 — Aug 12–18: Second Paid Conversion

**Outreach:**
- [ ] 2nd paid conversion
- [ ] Begin outreach to next batch of restaurants (paid, not design partners)

**Milestone:** 2 paying restaurants. MRR ≥ ₹7,000.

---

## Week 12 — Aug 19–25: Scale Outreach

**Outreach:**
- [ ] Pitch 3rd paid conversion
- [ ] Cold outreach to 10 new restaurants (WhatsApp + walk-in)
- [ ] Refine pitch based on what's working

**Milestone:** 3 paying restaurants. MRR ≥ ₹10,500.

---

## Week 13 — Aug 26–31: End of Summer Checkpoint

**Build:**
- [ ] Case study finalized (with restaurant's permission)
- [ ] Any critical Phase 2 features scoped and planned

**Outreach:**
- [ ] Push for 4th and 5th paying conversion
- [ ] Document outreach process for scaling post-summer

**Milestone:**
- [ ] 5 restaurants active (any mix of design partner / paid)
- [ ] MRR ≥ ₹35,000 (target: 5 paying × ₹7,000 average)
- [ ] Case study written and published on portfolio
- [ ] Enough data to tell the story in a placement interview
