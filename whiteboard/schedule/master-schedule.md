# Master Schedule — Summer 2026

> This is the canonical daily structure. Adjust times to fit your natural rhythm, but keep the block order and proportions the same.

---

## Daily Time Blocks

| Time | Block | What happens |
|------|-------|-------------|
| 06:30 – 08:00 | Workout + morning routine | Gym, walk, or workout at home. Non-negotiable. |
| 08:00 – 12:00 | **Deep Work 1 — Loop** | 4 hours. Build or outreach. No phone, no distraction. |
| 12:00 – 13:00 | Lunch + real break | Eat, rest, walk. Don't work. |
| 13:00 – 15:00 | **Deep Work 2 — Jobs** | Applications (30/day pace) + founder outreach (1/day) |
| 15:00 – 17:00 | **Freelance delivery** | Client work, calls, deliverables. Max 2 hrs. Hard stop. |
| 17:00 – 18:00 | **DSA** | 3 problems. Timed. No looking at solution until 20 min in. |
| 18:00 – 18:30 | **CS review / System design / Behavioral** | Rotating (see weekly split below) |
| 18:30 – 21:00 | Life | Tea, movie, gym social, walk, family. Offline when possible. |
| 21:00 – 21:30 | **Daily log + plan tomorrow** | Fill in daily-template.md. 10 min max. |

---

## Loop Block Split (alternating by day)

| Day | Deep Work 1 focus |
|-----|------------------|
| Monday | Build (code, features, tests) |
| Tuesday | Outreach (walk-ins, WhatsApp, follow-ups) |
| Wednesday | Build |
| Thursday | Outreach + design partner check-in |
| Friday | Build + deploy/fix |
| Saturday | Catch-up (whichever of build/outreach is behind) |
| Sunday | Rest, weekly review, planning |

When a restaurant onboarding session is scheduled, it takes priority over the Build/Outreach split for that day.

---

## 18:00–18:30 Rotating Focus

| Day | Focus |
|-----|-------|
| Monday | CS fundamentals review (Bucket 1 — 30 min) |
| Tuesday | System design topic (1 topic/week — see plan) |
| Wednesday | Behavioral story practice (pick 1, say it out loud) |
| Thursday | LinkedIn engage + content draft for tomorrow |
| Friday | Review week: what shipped, what didn't |
| Saturday | Free / spill-over |
| Sunday | Weekly review (see weekly-template.md) |

---

## Sunday: Weekly Review (30 min)

Use [weekly-template.md](./weekly-template.md).

Do this every Sunday evening before planning the next week:
1. Fill in numbers (applications sent, problems solved, Loop progress)
2. Answer: what worked, what didn't, what to adjust
3. Confirm next week's Loop focus (what to build or which restaurants to contact)
4. Set Monday's single most important task for each pillar

---

## Rules

1. **Deep Work 1 (Loop) starts at 8am.** Not after one more YouTube video. 8am.
2. **Freelance hard stops at 17:00.** If it's not done by then, it's a scope problem. Fix the scope.
3. **DSA is daily.** 3 problems. Not "I'll do 6 tomorrow." Today's problems are today's problems.
4. **Log is filled before bed.** 10 minutes. If you skipped it, you don't know where you are.
5. **Life time is real.** Don't feel guilty about the 18:30–21:00 block. It's in the plan. Take it.

---

## What to Do When You're Behind

If you missed a day or had a slow week:
- Don't try to "catch up" by sacrificing sleep or life time
- Adjust the **next 7 days' plan** (not the schedule structure)
- Ask: what specifically slipped? Why? Is it scope, distraction, or energy?
- Fix the root cause. Don't add more hours.

The schedule is designed to be sustainable for 13 weeks, not maximum output for 2 weeks.
