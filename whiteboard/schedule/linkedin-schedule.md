# LinkedIn Schedule — One System, Four Purposes

> LinkedIn serves all 4 pillars simultaneously: Loop marketing, job hunt, freelance, and network building. Run it as a system, not randomly.
> Full profile content spec (word-for-word, every field) → `blueprints/05-linkedin.md`

---

## The Four Purposes and Who You're Reaching

| Purpose | Target audience | What they need to see |
|---------|----------------|----------------------|
| Loop marketing | Restaurant owners, food tech founders | You're building Loop. It's real. You understand the problem. |
| Job hunt | Startup founders, CTOs, recruiters | Full-stack engineer with shipped products. Available. |
| Freelance | SMB owners, NGO directors, school operators | You build operational software that solves business problems. |
| Network | Builders, PMs, engineers, mentors | You're building in public. Thoughtful. Worth following. |

One piece of content can serve multiple audiences. No separate personas needed.

## Daily Routine (20–30 min total)

### Morning (10 min) — runs during 08:00 startup before deep work
1. Accept relevant connection requests
2. Reply to any comments on your posts (within 24 hrs, always)
3. Leave 3 meaningful comments on posts from your target audience:
   - 1 on a post by a founder or builder (job + network)
   - 1 on a post about restaurants / food tech / SaaS (Loop)
   - 1 on a post about SMB tech, India tech, or operations (freelance)

**What counts as meaningful:** 2–3 sentences. Add a perspective, ask a question, or share a related experience. Never "Great post!" — it's invisible and wastes your visibility.

### Evening (10–15 min) — fold into 21:00–21:30 daily log block
- Send 3–5 targeted connection requests (use templates below)
- Every other day: 1–2 follow-up messages to connections accepted in the last 3 days

## Posting Schedule (3–4 posts per week)

Post between **7–9am IST or 12–1pm IST** for maximum reach.
Always end with a question — drives comments → boosts distribution.

| Day | Type | Purpose |
|-----|------|---------|
| Monday | Building in public — Loop progress | Loop + Job hunt |
| Wednesday | Problem insight — a problem you're solving | Freelance + Network |
| Friday | Result or learning — a number, reaction, decision | All 4 |
| (Optional) Sunday | Week recap | Job hunt + Network |

**Content compounding rule:** every post should link or reference something useful — portfolio, a GitHub repo, Loop's page, or your About section. Always bring people back.

### Monday: Building in Public (Loop)
```
I'm building Loop — a WhatsApp loyalty tool for Indian restaurants.

This week I shipped [feature/milestone].

Here's what I learned:

[2–3 sentences — a non-obvious decision, a problem you hit, something that surprised you]

[Optional: screenshot or short video]
```

**Example:**
```
I'm building Loop — a WhatsApp loyalty tool for Indian restaurants.

This week I finished the multi-tenant RLS setup on Supabase. Every restaurant's 
customer data is isolated at the database level, not the application layer. 
Took longer than expected — but if there's ever a bug in the app code, 
the data isolation holds regardless.

The insight: security at the wrong layer gives you false confidence.

Looking for 5 restaurants in Bangalore to pilot free. DM if interested.

#buildinginpublic #supabase #saas #restauranttech
```

## Connection Request Strategy (5–10 per day)

Specific notes get 30–40% acceptance. Generic notes get 10–15%.

### Target 1: Founders at Indian startups (job hunt)
**Search:** "Founder" + "India" + your target industry

```
Hi [Name], I'm a full-stack engineer building Loop (WhatsApp loyalty SaaS for 
restaurants). Your work at [Company] on [specific thing] caught my attention — 
would love to connect.
```

**Search:** "restaurant owner Bangalore", "F&B founder India"

## Weekly Analytics Check (Sunday, 5 min)

LinkedIn → Profile → Analytics tab. Log every Sunday in `tracking/weekly-reports/week-NN.md`:

```
LinkedIn — Week [N]:
- Posts published: N
- Total impressions: N
- Profile views: N
- Search appearances: N
- New connections: N
- DM conversations: N
- Leads/opportunities: [describe any]
```

If impressions grow week over week → content is working.
If flat → change one variable: topic, format (add screenshot), or post time.

## Content Bank: Topics to Write About

Pick from this when you're stuck:

**Loop / building:**
- Why I chose WhatsApp over email for a loyalty product
- The one database decision that changed my architecture (RLS)
- How I'm finding my first restaurant customers (cold walk-ins)
- What I got wrong in the first version of [feature]
- Why independent restaurants are the right starting market
- The first time a restaurant owner said yes — what happened

**Technical:**
- What 55+ LeetCode problems taught me that courses didn't
- The hardest bug I fixed this week and how I found it
- Why I use Supabase for production apps
- What shipping a production e-commerce app in college actually taught me
- How I implemented spaced repetition (SM-2) in Synthesis

**Freelance / SMB tech:**
- Why most small businesses in India need operations software, not marketing
- What I learned approaching restaurants and tutoring centres cold
- The difference between a client who pays and a client who doesn't

**Observations:**
- Something you noticed about how restaurants operate in India
- A customer conversation that changed how you think about Loop
- A rejection or failure and what you did with it
- A decision you made that turned out to be right — and why
