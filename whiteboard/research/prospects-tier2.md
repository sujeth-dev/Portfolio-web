## How to Work Tier 2 — The Only Approach That Works

| Step | Action | Week |
|------|--------|------|
| 1 | Ask your network: "Do you know anyone running a manufacturing business or real estate firm in Bangalore?" | W2 |
| 2 | If yes → warm intro call. If no → LinkedIn comment on director's posts (don't DM cold) | W2–3 |
| 3 | Frame as research: "I'm understanding how [manufacturers/developers] manage operations — can I have 30 min?" | W3 |
| 4 | Discovery meeting (NOT a sales call). Ask: what's your biggest operational pain? what does it cost you? | W4–5 |
| 5 | Come back with a specific proposal — what you understood, what you'd build, timeline, price | W6+ |

**Tofler trick:** tofler.in → search any company → get director name + DIN + financials. Use director name to find on LinkedIn before calling.
**CA route:** One intro from a CA = trusted entry. If you know any CA, ask: "Do any of your clients run a manufacturing or real estate business?"
