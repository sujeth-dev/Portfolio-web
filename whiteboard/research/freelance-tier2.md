## How to Get In (Week 2 Action)

**This week's single action:** Don't pitch. Make one contact.

- Check if anyone in your personal network (family, college, LinkedIn 1st-degree) knows someone who runs a manufacturing business or real estate firm in Bangalore.
- Ask one person: *"Do you know anyone who runs a manufacturing business / real estate firm in Bangalore? I'm doing research on how they manage operations."*
- Frame it as research, not sales.

**Peenya Industries Association:** Visit piapeenya.com — member directory available. Look for engineering/fabrication companies with 30–100 employees. These are the ideal Tier 2 entry points.

**Karnataka Industries Online:** karnatakaindustriesonline.com — searchable directory of Karnataka manufacturers. Filter by Bangalore + sector.
