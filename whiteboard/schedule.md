# Schedule — Summer 2026

> The canonical daily structure. Keep the block order; adjust times to your rhythm.

---

## Daily Time Blocks

| Time | Block | What happens |
|------|-------|-------------|
| 06:30 – 08:00 | Workout + morning routine | Non-negotiable. |
| 08:00 – 12:00 | **Deep Work 1 — Loop** | 4 hrs. Build or outreach. No phone, no distraction. |
| 12:00 – 13:00 | Lunch + real break | Eat, rest, walk. Don't work. |
| 13:00 – 15:00 | **Deep Work 2 — Jobs** | Work through today's research queue (jobs file) + founder outreach |
| 15:00 – 17:00 | **Freelance delivery** | Client work, calls, deliverables. Hard stop at 17:00. |
| 17:00 – 18:00 | **DSA** | 3 problems. Timed. No solution until 20 min in. |
| 18:00 – 18:30 | **CS / System design / Behavioral** | Rotating focus (see below) |
| 18:30 – 21:00 | Life | Tea, movie, walk, family. Offline when possible. |
| 21:00 – 21:30 | **Daily log + plan tomorrow** | Fill `logs/daily/YYYY-MM-DD.md`. 10 min max. |

---

## Day Modes

**Mode A — Full day** (high energy, no interruptions)
Standard schedule as above.

**Mode B — Reduced day** (low energy, minor disruption, social obligation)
- Loop: 2 hrs — single most important task only
- Jobs: 1 hr — minimum 3 applications from research queue
- Freelance: delivery only (2 hrs)
- DSA: 1 problem — keeps the streak alive
- Skip: 18:00–18:30 rotating block

**Mode C — Off / Emergency day** (sick, travel, crisis, mental reset)
- Protect ONE thing: DSA (1 problem, 20 min) — the streak is the only KPI today
- Log the day as Mode C. No guilt. No mandatory catch-up.
- On Sunday `progress week`: if gap > 5 apps or > 3 DSA problems, redistribute to remaining days.

## Rules

1. **Deep Work 1 starts at 8am.** Not after one more video. 8am.
2. **Freelance hard stops at 17:00.** If it's not done, it's a scope problem.
3. **DSA is daily.** 3 problems on Mode A. 1 problem minimum on Mode B or C.
4. **Log before bed.** 10 minutes. If you skipped it, you don't know where you are.
5. **Life time is real.** The 18:30–21:00 block is in the plan. Take it.

---

## When You're Behind

- Don't sacrifice sleep or life time to catch up
- Check `progress week` to see the gap clearly
- Redistribute missing work across remaining days — don't add more hours
- Fix the root cause (scope, distraction, energy), not the symptom
