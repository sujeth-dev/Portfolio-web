## End of Summer Audit Questions

1. **Loop:** Are real restaurants using Loop today? How many? What did you learn?
2. **Jobs:** If placement season started tomorrow, would you feel ready? Do you have leads?
3. **DSA:** Can you solve 3 LeetCode mediums in 1 hour without panic?
4. **Freelance:** Is the income stable? Did the outreach experiments teach you anything about client acquisition?
5. **LinkedIn:** Has building in public helped you? Who has it connected you with?

**The test:** Can you walk into a placement interview and say:
> "I built Loop, a WhatsApp loyalty SaaS now used by [N] restaurants in Bangalore. I designed the architecture, did the sales, onboarded the customers, and support it monthly. Here's what I learned."

If yes — you've won this summer.
