## Section 3: Level-Up Path (Month-by-Month Analytics Targets)

Open LinkedIn Analytics weekly: Profile → Analytics → check every Sunday.

| Metric | Month 1 (June) | Month 2 (July) | Month 3 (Aug) |
|--------|---------------|---------------|--------------|
| Profile views/week | 20+ | 50+ | 100+ |
| Search appearances/week | 50+ | 200+ | 500+ |
| Post impressions per post (avg) | 200+ | 500+ | 1,000+ |
| New connections/week | 30–50 | 20–30 | 15–20 |
| DM conversations/week | 1–2 | 3–5 | 5–10 |
| Real opportunities traced | 0 (baseline) | 1 | 2+ |

**Diagnostics:**
- Profile views flat after 3 weeks → add more keywords to headline or About
- Post impressions flat → change one variable: posting time (8am vs 12pm IST), or add a screenshot/diagram
- Connection acceptance below 25% → messages are too generic, make them more specific to the person

**What counts as a "real opportunity":** A DM, intro, referral, or interview you can trace directly to a post or LinkedIn connection. Log these in your weekly report.

## Section 4: Recommendations Strategy

Target: 3 recommendations on profile by end of July. Recruiters and founders read these.

**Who to ask:**
1. Owner of Zingara Restaurant or Fitness Garage — ask for 3–5 sentences about the work you did
2. A co-lead from E-Cell UVCE — they can speak to leadership and execution over 2 years
3. A college peer or professor who knows your technical work

**Script (WhatsApp or DM):**
> "Hey [Name], hope you're well! I'm actively applying to jobs this summer and wondered if you'd be comfortable writing me a short LinkedIn recommendation — just 3–5 sentences about what we built together / how you found working with me. It would mean a lot. Here's my LinkedIn: [link]"

**Offer to reciprocate.** Writing theirs first increases the chance they write yours.

## Section 5: DM Conversion Playbook

When someone responds to a connection request, comments on a post, or DMs you:

**Scenario A — Founder/recruiter who liked or commented on your post**
> "Hey [Name], thanks for the [comment/like] — glad it resonated. I'm building Loop right now and actively looking at SWE roles and founder conversations. Would you be open to a 15-minute chat sometime?"

**Scenario B — Restaurant owner who connected after a Loop post**
> "Hi [Name], thanks for connecting! I'm piloting Loop free for 3 months with select Bangalore restaurants — takes 30 min to set up, runs on WhatsApp. Would you be open to a quick call this week to see if it's a fit?"

**Scenario C — Engineer or builder who connected**
> "Hey [Name], building in public is underrated. What are you working on right now?"

**Scenario D — Recruiter who sent an InMail about a role**
> "Hi [Name], thanks for reaching out — this looks interesting. I'm a final-year AIML student at UVCE, currently shipping Loop (B2B SaaS, live, real customers). Happy to do a quick screen. What's the process?"

**Rule:** Every DM gets a reply within 24 hrs. Every accepted connection gets a follow-up within 3 days if there's a relevant reason.
