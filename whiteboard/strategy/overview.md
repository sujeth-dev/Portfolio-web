# Summer 2026 — Master Operating System

> **Start:** 4 June 2026 | **End:** 31 August 2026 | **Duration:** 13 weeks
> **Mission:** Enter placement season with a live SaaS used by real restaurants, an active interview pipeline, and proof that you can build, ship, sell, and support a real product.

---

## The Four Pillars

| # | Pillar | What it produces | Daily time |
|---|--------|-----------------|------------|
| 1 | **Loop** (Loyalty Platform) | Live product + paying restaurants | 4 hrs |
| 2 | **Job Pipeline** | Applications + active interview processes | 2 hrs |
| 3 | **Placement Readiness** | DSA solved + interview-ready projects | 1 hr |
| 4 | **Freelance** | Consistent cashflow + retained clients | 1–2 hrs |

**When time is short, drop in this order:** Freelance → DSA → Jobs → Loop. Loop is never optional.

---

## Three Phases

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Jun 4–30)
Get everything live and running. No excuses, no delays.

- Loop: MVP1 build complete, deployed, first restaurant contacted
- Jobs: Resume done, LinkedIn done, tracker built, 20 applications sent
- DSA: Routine established, 20+ problems done this month
- Freelance: Clients confirmed, deliverables on schedule

### Phase 2 — Build & Ship (Jul 1–31)
Operate at full capacity. Push hard.

- Loop: 3 restaurants live, first paid conversation happening
- Jobs: 150 applications sent, 50 founder outreaches, first interview
- DSA: 80+ problems done this month, patterns solidifying
- Freelance: Consistent delivery, one new client explored

### Phase 3 — Proof & Leverage (Aug 1–31)
Convert effort into evidence. Enter placement season with leverage.

- Loop: 5 restaurants active, ₹35k+ MRR, case study written
- Jobs: 250+ applications total, active interview processes
- DSA: 200 problems total, mock interviews done, project defense ready
- Freelance: Stable, portfolio case studies written
