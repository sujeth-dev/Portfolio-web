## Tier 1: SMB Operational SaaS — Industries to Experiment With

### The filter: does this business already have demand but operational chaos?

If yes → they'll pay monthly forever because the pain is daily. You're not selling them marketing. You're selling them control.

---

### Industry List + What to Build + Who to Contact

#### 1. Schools and Tutoring Centres
**Their problem:** Fee collection is manual (WhatsApp reminders), attendance is a register, parent communication is calls, results are Excel.
**What you sell:** Fee management + attendance + parent portal. One system.
**Monthly price:** ₹3,000–7,000/month depending on student count
**Who decides:** Owner/principal (usually runs it directly)
**Where to find them:**
- Google Maps: "coaching centre [your area]", "tuition centre Koramangala"
- JustDial search for "school management software Bangalore" — businesses searching this already have the pain
- Walk into any coaching centre near your home — owner is usually there 4–8pm
**Best approach:** Walk-in or cold call. Direct.

---

#### 2. Field Service Businesses (AC repair, pest control, plumbers, electricians)
**Their problem:** Jobs come on WhatsApp, scheduling is chaos, invoicing is handwritten, follow-ups are forgotten, no one knows which technician is where.
**What you sell:** Job management + invoicing + technician tracking + follow-up automation.
**Monthly price:** ₹2,500–6,000/month
**Who decides:** Business owner (usually handles operations directly)
**Where to find them:**
- Google: "AC service Bangalore", "pest control Bangalore" — call the ones with 10+ reviews (big enough to have the problem)
- JustDial business listings for "home services Bangalore"
- Ask in local housing society WhatsApp groups — they always know these vendors
**Best approach:** Cold call + follow-up WhatsApp

---

#### 3. Pharmacies (independent, not chains)
**Their problem:** Stock expiry is missed, reorder is guesswork, prescription records are paper, supplier payments tracked in notebook.
**What you sell:** Inventory + expiry alerts + supplier tracking. Not billing (they already have billing software). Just inventory intelligence.
**Monthly price:** ₹2,000–4,000/month
**Who decides:** Pharmacist-owner
**Where to find them:**
- Walk into any independent pharmacy (not Apollo, not MedPlus). Ask for the owner.
- Google Maps: "medical store [locality]"
**Best approach:** Walk-in. They're always at the counter.

---

#### 4. Equipment and Asset Rental
**Their problem:** Who has what equipment, when is it due back, what's damaged, who owes money — all tracked in notebooks or WhatsApp.
**What you sell:** Rental tracking (issue, return, damage, deposit, overdue alerts).
**Monthly price:** ₹3,000–6,000/month
**Who decides:** Owner
**Where to find them:**
- Google: "tent house Bangalore", "event equipment rental", "construction equipment rental"
- IndiaMART listings for "equipment rental Bangalore"
**Best approach:** Cold WhatsApp message after finding number on Google listing

---

#### 5. Cooperative Societies / Apartment Associations
**Their problem:** Maintenance collection is manual, defaulters are chased on WhatsApp, expenses are tracked in Excel, member records are outdated.
**What you sell:** Maintenance collection + member management + expense tracking + notices.
**Monthly price:** ₹2,000–5,000/month (paid from society funds — easy to approve)
**Who decides:** Secretary or treasurer
**Where to find them:**
- Housing society apps (NoBrokerHood, ApnaComplex) — these associations are everywhere
- Post in local resident WhatsApp groups: "I build custom management tools for housing societies"
- LinkedIn: search "apartment association secretary Bangalore"
**Best approach:** LinkedIn message + referral through personal network

---

#### 6. NGOs / Nonprofits (mid-size)
**Their problem:** Donor records are Excel, fund disbursement is manual, impact reporting is painful, grant compliance needs proof.
**What you sell:** Donor management + disbursement tracking + impact dashboard.
**Monthly price:** ₹3,000–8,000/month (funders often mandate systems — budget already exists)
**Who decides:** Executive Director or Program Manager
**Where to find them:**
- GiveIndia, CAF India, Credibility Alliance — NGOs listed there are serious organisations
- LinkedIn: "NGO Bangalore" + filter by size 10-50 employees
- Google: "NGO Bangalore [cause area]" — find mid-size ones with active websites
**Best approach:** LinkedIn + cold email (they respond to email better than walk-ins)

### The Experiment Plan: First 4 Weeks

Don't try all 6 at once. Pick 2 industries, run hard for 4 weeks, measure, then decide.

**Recommended starting pair:**
- **Schools/tutoring centres** (most accessible, owner reachable, immediate pain)
- **Field service businesses** (high daily pain, low existing solutions)

**Week 1:** Research 10 targets in each industry. Build a target list with name, location, contact method.
**Week 2:** 5 outreaches per industry per week (total 10/week). Mix walk-in + cold call + WhatsApp.
**Week 3:** Follow up on all Week 2 contacts. Add 5 new per industry.
**Week 4:** Review: which industry got the most responses? Which conversations went furthest? **Double down on that one.**

Track every outreach in a simple log:
| Date | Business | Industry | Method | Response | Next action |

## Tier 1 Outreach Methods

### Method 1: Walk-in (highest conversion for local businesses)
**Best for:** Schools, pharmacies, field service businesses
**When:** Weekday 10am–12pm or 3pm–5pm (owners available, not peak)
**Script:**
> "Hi, I'm Sujeth — I build software for businesses like yours. I noticed [specific pain — "most schools I talk to are still collecting fees on WhatsApp"]. I've built a system that handles [X] — would you be open to a 10-minute chat?"

If yes → show a demo (have a prototype or screenshots ready)
If busy → "No problem. Can I get your WhatsApp to send you a quick video?"

---

### Method 2: Cold WhatsApp (for businesses with public numbers)
**Best for:** Field service, equipment rental, pharmacies
**Template:**
```
Hi [Name],

I'm Sujeth, a software developer in Bangalore. I build management tools for 
[industry] businesses.

Quick question: do you track [jobs/rentals/stock] on WhatsApp or paper right now?

If yes, I've built a simple system that handles [X] for ₹[Y]/month. 
Takes 30 min to set up.

Happy to show you a demo this week — just 15 minutes.
```

**Follow-up (if no response in 3 days):**
```
Hi [Name], just following up on my message. Happy to adjust if the timing isn't right.
```

One follow-up only. If no response, move on.

---

**Best for:** NGO directors, housing society executives, SME owners with LinkedIn presence
**Connection request note:**

### Method 5: Referral (most underused, highest conversion)
Ask every current client: "Do you know anyone running a [school/clinic/NGO] who might have similar challenges?"
One sentence ask. Warm leads close 3–5x faster than cold.

## Tier 2: High-Ticket Custom Software — One Parallel Pursuit

Pick ONE industry from this list and pursue it as a long-game over the summer:

| Industry | Their problem | Your build | Ticket size |
|----------|-------------|------------|-------------|
| Mid-size manufacturer (garments, food processing) | Production tracking + inventory + dispatch | Custom manufacturing OS | ₹8L–30L + ₹40-80k/month |
| Real estate developer (active projects) | Payment schedules + milestone tracking + buyer portal | Custom project management | ₹8L–25L + retainer |
| Jewellery chain (5+ outlets) | Weight tracking, karigar job work, purity records | Custom jewellery management | ₹10L–50L |
| Franchise business (20+ outlets) | Outlet performance, royalties, compliance, inventory | Franchise OS | ₹10L–40L + recurring |
| Staffing/manpower company (500+ workers) | Payroll across clients, PF/ESI compliance, attendance | HR + compliance system | ₹10L–30L + recurring |

**Recommended starting vertical for you: Mid-size manufacturer or Franchise business**
- Bangalore has Peenya + Bommasandra industrial areas with hundreds of manufacturers
- Franchise segment is booming in India (cloud kitchens, salon chains, education)

### How to get into Tier 2 (this is a 2–3 month process)

**Step 1: Find the entry point (Week 1–2)**
You cannot cold call your way to a ₹20L deal. Entry is via:
- CA / accountant referral — they know which businesses are bleeding from bad systems AND they're trusted
- Industry association — Manufacturers Association of Karnataka, CREDAI (real estate), Retailers Association
- One family/college connection in that industry — even one warm intro is worth 50 cold calls

**Your action:** Ask your network: "Do you know anyone who runs a manufacturing business / franchise / jewellery store in Bangalore?" You need one introduction.

**Step 2: Discovery meeting (not a sales call)**
Frame it as: "I'm doing research on how [manufacturers/franchises] manage their operations. Could I come in and understand your workflow for 30 minutes?"

You are NOT selling anything yet. You're understanding the problem.

Questions to ask:
- "How do you currently track [orders/production/inventory]?"
- "What's the most painful part of running [X] operations?"
- "What does getting that wrong cost you?" (Get them to name a number)
- "Have you looked at software for this? What happened?"

**Step 3: Proposal (Week 6+)**
After understanding the problem deeply, come back with:
- What you understood about their pain
- What you'd build (high-level)
- Timeline (3 months typical for a proper system)
- Price (don't underquote — ₹5L minimum for a serious system)
- Ongoing support fee (₹30-60k/month)

**Step 4: Close (this takes 4–8 weeks from first meeting)**
These clients need multiple touchpoints. Follow up every 10 days. Bring a prototype. Show progress. They buy confidence, not just code.

## Client Retention Rules

- **Never miss a self-set deadline.** If you will miss it, tell the client 48 hrs before.
- **Friday update message** to every retainer client (template below). Non-negotiable.
- **Monthly review call** (15 min): ask "Is this still solving the right problem?"

**Friday update template:**
```
Hi [Name],

Week update:
Done: [what you completed]
In progress: [what's underway]
Next week: [what you'll work on]

Any changes or questions? Let me know by Monday.
— Sujeth
```

---

## Rate and Invoicing

- **Invoice on 1st of every month** for retainer clients
- **Invoice at milestone delivery** for project clients
- **Follow up unpaid invoices** after 7 days (one WhatsApp, then one call)
- **Rate for new clients:** Never start below market rate. Bangalore market for freelance full-stack: ₹800–1,500/hr or ₹15,000–50,000 per project depending on scope
