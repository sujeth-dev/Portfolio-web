# Blueprint: Job Pipeline — Dual-Track Machine

> Two tracks running simultaneously. Campus = safety net. Direct = primary. Both require consistent daily action starting Week 2.

---

## The Two Tracks

### Track A: Campus Placements (Safety Net)
- **Timeline:** Placement season starts Aug/Sept 2026
- **Process:** Register with placement cell → OA → Group Discussion → Technical Interview → HR
- **Companies:** Tier 1 product companies + funded startups visiting campus
- **Your edge:** Loop as a live product. Most candidates have projects. You have a business.
- **Action required now:** Register with placement cell immediately. Update college placement profile. Don't wait.

### Track B: Direct Applications (Primary)
- **Timeline:** Start Week 2 (Jun 9). Run continuously until placement season.
- **Process:** Apply → follow-up → phone screen → technical interview → offer
- **Target:** Series A–C startups, product companies, 10–150 employees
- **Volume:** 5–7 applications/day, 30–40/week
- **Your edge:** Full-stack + shipped products + can speak to business outcomes

## Direct Applications: Where to Apply

### Platform 1: LinkedIn (volume + brand visibility)
- Use "Easy Apply" for bulk applications (filter: Full Stack, SDE, Product Engineer, India, 10-200 employees)
- Customize the 3-line note for any company you actually care about
- Apply first thing in the morning (applications reviewed in AM)
- Set up job alerts: "Full Stack Engineer", "Product Engineer", "SDE-I" — India, Remote

### Platform 2: Wellfound / AngelList (startup-focused)
- Best for Series A–C funded startups
- Companies often have founders directly reviewing applications
- Complete your Wellfound profile fully (it acts as your resume there)
- Filter: Full-stack, India or Remote, funded in last 2 years

### Platform 3: YC / accelerator job boards
- jobs.ycombinator.com — filter by India, Engineering
- Check Sequoia India, Accel India portfolio company pages
- Google: `site:lever.co "full stack" "India"` or `site:greenhouse.io "full stack" "Bangalore"`

### Platform 4: Internshala (PPO route)
- Apply for 6-month internships at product companies → convert to PPO
- Useful if you want guaranteed return offer before placements begin
- Focus: startups offering stipend ₹25k+ that have PPO history

### Platform 5: Direct company websites
- Find companies via Google: "[startup name] careers" or "[company] engineering blog"
- Check LinkedIn company page → Jobs tab
- Target: companies you'd genuinely want to work at — apply directly via their site

## What to Apply For

### Target roles (use these exact strings when searching):
- "Full Stack Engineer"
- "Product Engineer"
- "Software Engineer"
- "SDE-I" or "SDE 1"
- "Backend Engineer" (if full-stack adjacent)

### Target companies:
- Series A–C funded Indian startups (Tracxn, Crunchbase, YC list)
- Product companies with engineering blogs (sign of good culture)
- B2B SaaS, fintech, healthtech, edtech, restaurant/food tech
- 10–150 employees (small enough for impact, big enough for structure)

### Filter to apply quickly (30-second decision rule):
1. Role title: contains "full stack" OR "product engineer" OR "SDE"? → continue
2. Company: funded startup or product company (not IT services/outsourcing)? → continue
3. Location: India / Remote / Bangalore? → continue
4. Company size: under 500 employees? → continue
5. Requirements: do you meet 60%+ of them? → **APPLY, stop overthinking**

### Hard skip:
- Pure IT services / outsourcing firms (TCS, Infosys, Wipro, HCL for engineering roles)
- Companies with "2+ years required" that won't consider freshers
- Roles where you'd only do frontend OR only backend (you want full-stack impact)

## Application: What to Write

### Cover note formula (3 sentences, customize per application):

**Sentence 1:** What you've built that's most relevant to them
**Sentence 2:** Why their specific problem or domain interests you
**Sentence 3:** The ask — one line, direct

**Template:**
```
I'm a full-stack engineer who shipped Loop, a WhatsApp-native loyalty SaaS 
currently being used by restaurants in Bangalore — built on Next.js, Supabase, 
and Razorpay. [Company]'s focus on [their domain] is the exact space I want to 
work in — I've had to solve similar [X] problems building Loop. Happy to show 
you the codebase or walk through the architecture in a call.
```

Swap in a different project lead (Synthesis for edtech companies, Possah for e-commerce companies).

**For companies you really want:** Don't use Easy Apply. Find the hiring manager or engineering lead on LinkedIn. Send a connection request + note.

## Founder Outreach (5–8 per week, starting Week 2)

This is separate from applications. You're not asking for a job — you're starting a conversation.

### Who to reach out to:
- Founders of B2B SaaS companies in India (10–50 employees)
- CTOs/engineering leads at restaurant tech, consumer apps, fintech startups
- Anyone building something adjacent to what you're building

### LinkedIn message template (connection note, 300 chars max):
```
Hi [Name], I'm building Loop — a WhatsApp loyalty SaaS for restaurants. 
Shipping MVP this month. Your work at [Company] on [X] caught my attention. 
Would love to connect and learn from your journey.
```

### LinkedIn follow-up (after connection accepted, 2–3 days later):
```
Thanks for connecting! Quick context: I'm a final-year CS student shipping 
a B2B SaaS product (restaurant loyalty, Bangalore-first). I'd love 15 minutes 
to hear how you approached [specific thing about their company]. No agenda 
beyond learning — would you be open to a quick call?
```

### Twitter/X DM (for founders who are active on X):
```
Hey [Name] — following your work on [Company]. I'm building Loop, a 
WhatsApp-native loyalty tool for restaurants. Just shipped MVP. Would love 
to hear how you handled early B2B sales. DM if open for a quick chat?
```

### Cold email (if you have their email):
```
Subject: Quick question from a builder in Bangalore

Hi [Name],

I'm Sujeth — final-year CS student building Loop, a WhatsApp loyalty SaaS 
for Bangalore restaurants. About to do my first outreach push this week.

I've been following [Company] — specifically how you approached [X]. 
Would you be open to a 15-minute call? I'm looking to learn from founders 
who've done early-stage B2B in India.

No ask beyond the conversation.
— Sujeth
```

**Track every outreach.** Note: sent date, platform, response (yes/no/pending).

## What "Progress" Looks Like

Good week: 30+ applications, 5+ outreaches, 1+ response of any kind
Great week: 40+ applications, 8+ outreaches, 1+ interview scheduled
Bad week: <20 applications, 0 outreaches — investigate why and fix

The pipeline is a numbers game at this stage. Rejection is not failure. No response is not failure. Volume + quality + persistence = interviews.
