Engineering Philosophy

Need is the driver.
Tech is what you reach for when you've
found the right problem.

Learning a technology, a language, a framework — all important. But knowing where to apply it matters more. Having a hundred tools you don't use is waste. Knowing two and applying them to the right problem, for the right person or business — that's where real impact actually lives.

Today I know how to build apps, websites, and full-stack systems. If tomorrow the problem calls for wiring up an IoT device or integrating hardware I've never touched, I'll figure it out. The goal isn't to already know — it's to get the problem solved, within real constraints of cost, time, and what's actually needed. Whatever it takes.
